- **Interest acquired:** 80% of the equity share capital
- **Consideration:** INR 690,000,000, paid in cash on completion by Northwind Inc.
  Northwind Inc. recorded the investment in USD at the spot rate on the completion date.

| Item | INR |
| --- | --- |
| Equity share capital | 250,000,000 |
| Reserves and surplus at 1 April 2024 | 410,000,000 |
| Profit for 1 April to 30 June 2024 | 18,000,000 |
| **Net assets at completion** | **678,000,000** |

- Fair values of identifiable assets and liabilities equal their book values. No fair value adjustments.
- Northwind India's financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March.
